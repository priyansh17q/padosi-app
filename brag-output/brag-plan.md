# Brag Plan: Padosi Pro

## What is this app?
A hyperlocal marketplace where verified neighbors post and pick up small local tasks — grocery runs, deliveries, quick favors — with escrow-protected UPI/card payments, live tracking, and an OTP handoff that releases payout instantly.

## The angle
This isn't a pitch — it's a "we shipped a real thing" reveal. The premise: everyone building gig-economy apps is trying to compete with strangers and dark stores. Padosi Pro instead makes the person three doors down the delivery network — and it's not a mockup, it's a working app with real escrow, real live tracking, real instant payout. The angle is confidence through demonstration: show the flow working, end on the tagline.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
Full-bleed emerald-to-dark gradient. Big type: "Your neighbor is the network." Beat, then it cuts straight into the live product — no long setup.

## Key moments (the middle)
- The Explore feed's live task cards — real ₹ amounts, real distance/time metadata, a "Quick Accept" tap
- Escrow locking in on Post Task — the total pinned at the bottom, the lock icon, "Post Task with Escrow"
- Live Tracking's radar-ping runner marker and the countdown ETA actually ticking down
- The OTP handoff PIN releasing payout straight to the Wallet balance

## Outro / punchline
Logo mark on the dark gradient background. Line: "Your neighbor is the network." Small line beneath: "Padosi Pro." Clean hold, no CTA scramble — confidence through restraint.

## User flow worth showing
Entry → key action → result, pulled straight from the real app:
1. **Entry:** Explore feed — "14 tasks within 1.5 km," a grocery-run card at ₹220 with a Quick Accept button.
2. **Key action:** Post Task screen — escrow total locks in (₹210), then Live Tracking opens: runner pin on the map, ETA ticking down, OTP PIN "5821" visible for handoff.
3. **Result:** Wallet screen — balance updates, "Withdraw to UPI" — payout lands.

## Tone
- Preset: app-store
- Creative direction: fintech-grade trust with neighborhood warmth — clean feature-card reveals, nothing loud, the product is treated as real and dependable (because it is).
- Interpretation: 4-6 scenes, title-case labels, one feature beat per scene, clean slide/wipe transitions (0.35-0.45s), no aggression — let the real screenshots and their existing polish carry the video.

## Format: landscape — 1920x1080
## Duration: 20s target

## Visual identity (from the project)
- Background: `#f8f9ff` (soft mint-white surface)
- Accent: `#006948` (primary emerald), with `#85f8c4` (primary-fixed mint) for highlight fills and `#825100`/`#ffb95f` (tertiary amber) used sparingly for urgency accents
- Text: `#0b1c30` (on-surface near-black)
- Display font: Plus Jakarta Sans (weight 700-800)
- Body font: Plus Jakarta Sans (weight 400-600) — single typeface throughout, matching the real app
- Strongest visual element: the Live Tracking screen — pulsing radar rings around a bouncing runner pin over a live map, with a ticking ETA countdown. This is the most "alive" moment in the actual product and should anchor the video's most energetic beat.

## Audio direction
- Role: warm, consistent light layer — confident, not hyped
- Music: `happy-beats-business-moves-vol-11-by-ende-dot-app.mp3` (warm, business-y, 114.84 BPM, fits app-store)
- Music treatment: starts at 0s under the hook at low volume (~0.3), holds steady through the feature beats, small volume lift into the outro, clean fade over the final ~1s
- Music cue guidance: preset read from `happy-beats-business-moves-vol-11-by-ende-dot-app.music-cues.md`. Strong cues available at 1.60s, 3.70s, 5.80s/6.34s, 8.96s/9.50s, 12.65s, 17.91s. Target: hook line lands near 1.60s; Explore-feed reveal near 3.70s; escrow-lock moment near 8.96-9.50s; live-tracking reveal near 12.65s; payout ding near 17.91s. Treat all as ±0.15s hints, not hard locks — never sacrifice readability for a cue.
- Audio-reactive treatment: subtle — let the emerald glow behind the logo and the radar-ring opacity breathe slightly with music RMS. No waveform/equalizer visuals, no strobing.
- SFX posture: consistent light layer per app-store guidance — `interface/drop_*` or `interface/click_*` on each feature-card reveal, one `impact/impactBell_heavy_000` on the outro logo. All SFX at 0.65-0.75 volume, music at ~0.3.
- Audio-coupled moments: Quick Accept tap (click), escrow total locking in (soft drop), OTP PIN reveal (light plate/notification ping), wallet balance updating (bell/chip sound), logo outro (bell hit).
- Restraint rule: no glitch/chaotic SFX, no aggressive hits — this is a trust product, the sound should feel dependable, not exciting-for-its-own-sake.
